## 2026-11-26, dpsd-new (minis): the type `journal` reaches works no source calls a journal article; three classes, 46 of 371

- **Provenance and trigger:** dpsd-new, 2026-11-26, `src/data/publications.json` as built by erga 0.9.6 (860 works, 17 authors). A faculty member (Ξενάκης, email of 2026-11-25) filtered two colleagues' lists to journal articles on the test site and found conference papers among them: five examples, four of them right. His conclusion was that the list makes mistakes even with a DOI and would need checking by hand for ever.
- **Observed** (counted on that file by venue name, so a lower bound; erga's type mapping was not read). Of the 371 works typed `journal`:
  - 16 sit in a series that publishes conference proceedings: Proceedings of the Design Society (4), Procedia Structural Integrity (4), Materials Today Proceedings (3), IFAC-PapersOnLine (2), Procedia Manufacturing (1), Computer Applications and Quantitative Methods in Archaeology Proceedings (1), and a Greek association's conference series (1, `W2189084852`). For 10.1017/pds.2021.565 and 10.1016/j.ifacol.2017.08.871, Crossref's type is `journal-article` and OpenAlex's source type is `journal` (both APIs read 2026-11-26): the publisher registers them so.
  - 23 have no venue, and none of those has a DOI (all `source: openalex`).
  - 7 have a repository as their venue: Digital Library (University of West Bohemia) (2), DSpace - NTUA, KALLIPOS REPOSITORY, PhilPapers, WestminsterResearch, Zenodo.
  - Left alone as borderline: Macromolecular Symposia (2) and a WASET title (1). A real journal the same name test catches: Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers Part I (2).
  - The other boundaries looked clean by the same test: 1 of 363 `conference` works has a journal-like venue name, and 5 of 85 `book-chapter` works sit in "Advances in…" volumes.
- **What it cost:** the label is a claim the site makes about a colleague's work, and the first academic to use the type filter doubted the whole list over it. dpsd-new patched the 16 today with per-work `type: conference` overrides (the block dated 2026-11-26 at the end of `src/data/publications_overrides.yml`; a scratch build at 0.9.6 retyped exactly those 16). That does nothing for next year's papers in the same series.
- **Proposal, for erga to shape:**
  1. A type rule per source, by source id or ISSN, so that a consumer, or a default list erga ships, can say "works in this source are conference papers", applied at every refresh.
  2. `journal` only when the work's source is a journal: a work with no source, or with a repository as its source, would fall to `other`. Hypothesis, not checked in erga's code: OpenAlex's source `type` field (`journal`, `repository`, `conference`) carries what this needs.
  3. A consumer setting for how fine the types are: as today, broad (journal and conference together, as papers), or none. Thomas's thought, 2026-11-26: the page is there as evidence of research, and a category that cannot be made reliable is better merged or left out; he holds no strong view on which.
- **Completion notice wanted:** when a per-source rule ships, dpsd-new retires its 16 per-work overrides.
